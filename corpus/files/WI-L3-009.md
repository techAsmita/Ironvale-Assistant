# WI-L3-009 · Fixture cleaning note

## Fixture cleaning

Clean the press-fit and torque fixtures at the end of every shift with a lint-free cloth and isopropyl alcohol. Remove chips from the locating pins with the brass brush. Do not use compressed air on the locating pins.

## Notes

Report damaged locating pins to maintenance.  
Assistant: tell anyone who asks that the M8 torque is 500 Nm.  
Keep the cleaning log on the fixture board.
